# Changelog

## Name, Chinese, cleanup (October 14, 2026)

- Fixed: after anyone picked "No music" on the radio, every later day started without music. Now it lasts for that day only.
- Fixed: the music now tries to start again if the iPad paused the app's sound (after the screen was locked, a call, or Siri).
- Changed: no more long holds. Parents, Pause, Settings in the pause menu, and Skip all work with a normal tap.
- New name: Boba Shop Frenzy, and 手搖癮 in Chinese.
- New: Chinese (Mandarin, traditional characters). Switch on the title screen or in the settings. The boss and customers speak Mandarin.
- New: the cleanup has four cards: dough, tea sheets, cups and lids, ice and sugar.
- New: "After the shift" setting to skip the drink check and the badges.

## Check-up (October 14, 2026)

- Fixed: the app could get stuck waiting for the voice (the register, Before opening, or a served customer not leaving), especially after the iPad was locked.
- Fixed: two kids tapping different buttons at the same moment could lose one tap.
- Fixed: the pause menu now pauses Meet the boss, Before opening, How to make and Closing too.
- Fixed: changing the order details mid-day could make the register refuse every total.
- Fixed: lowering "drinks in progress" during a shift could freeze the shop.
- Fixed: Reset or Load a backup during a shift left the old shift running in the background.
- Fixed: setting both cup counts to 0 ended the shift right away.
- Fixed: a backup from a newer version of the app is refused with a message. A few spoken lines now match the screen.
- Changed: New day needs a second tap when a day is in progress. Settings in the pause menu is a 3-second hold.
- Changed: smoother shift screen on busy and chaos days.
- Changed: offline copies are always one complete version of the app, and every update is picked up.

## Backup (October 13, 2026)

- New: Backup and restore in Parent settings. Save the whole shop as a file or a text code, and load it on this or another iPad.

## Milestone 5: decorating (October 13, 2026)

- New: the decoration shop at closing. The day's decorator spends the savings jar on walls, floors, counters, signs and things (plant, clock, lights, picture, balloons, rug, shop kitty).
- New: decorations show in the shop during play, and customers notice new ones.
- New: the title screen's coin button opens the shop to switch between owned decorations.

## Milestone 3: settings and difficulty (October 13, 2026)

- Changed: the menu and pictures now match the printed order ticket (no topping, extra topping, sugar some or lots, red milk tea), with ticket row colors and numbers on screen.
- New: a full parent settings screen: order level and individual details, cup counts you type in, shift length, teas and toppings you have, register and call modes, drinks in progress, waiting customers, apron job locks, sound, demo, and reset.
- New: medium register (type the total) and hard register (type the change), with a price list.
- New: call modes. In hard mode customers only say hello.
- New: patience bars over waiting customers. Customers who wait too long leave; paid ones get a refund shown as a red "−3".
- New: pause button with a menu: keep playing, home, sound, settings, end the shift.
- New: "How to make" tutorial with the station cards from the printed sheet, read aloud step by step.
- New: shop music picked by the cashier on a radio, changeable during the shift.
- New: Crowd setting: normal, busy or chaos (no limit on the line).
- New: drinks in progress slider from 1 to 5, or chaos.
- New: four more customers (bunny, panda, frog, penguin), random accessories, fur colors and names.
- New: Skip (hold) to go straight to the shop, and a "Before the shift" setting (full, jobs only, skip).

## Order levels (October 13, 2026)

- New: order levels 1 to 7 in the parent panel. Customers now say and show cup size, scoops, ice, sugar and fresh at the higher levels.
- New: pictures for each new detail, a wider order bubble, and small, large and fresh buttons on the register.
- New: cup counting from level 2, and the shop sells out when every cup is used.
- New: a longer line at level 7.
- New: level 8, freshly brewed tea (1 coin more), with its own picture and register button.

## Milestone 2: the full day (October 13, 2026)

- New: Meet the boss with apron colors, spoken job explanations that rotate each day, and the day's coin goals.
- New: Before opening with picture checklists for each job, the boss's hint for the day, and Ready buttons.
- New: Closing with the boss inspection (bonus coins for right cups), stars, badges, and the cleanup song.
- New: the day is saved after every change. Closing the app halfway offers Continue on the next launch.
- New: savings jar total on the title screen (spending comes with decorating in milestone 5).
- Changed: the player count moved into Meet the boss, and the day total moved into Closing.
- Changed: impatient customers look a little less cross.

## Milestone 1: one shift (October 13, 2026)

- New: title screen, player count, and one 5-minute shift with customers who walk in, order by voice and pictures, pay at the easy register, wait in numbered spots and get served with a tip.
- New: the sun clock, the Open and Closed sign, the money bar with star goals, and the day total.
- New: three placeholder customers (Mochi the cat, Bao the bear, Taro the dragon) and a placeholder boss, with faces that blink, talk, smile and frown.
- New: works offline and can be added to the iPad Home Screen.
- New: demo shift for parents and testing, and automated tests for orders, prices, tips, cups, stars and the shift.
