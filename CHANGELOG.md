# Changelog

## Order levels (October 13, 2026)

- New: order levels 1 to 7 in the parent panel. Customers now say and show cup size, scoops, ice, sugar and fresh at the higher levels.
- New: pictures for each new detail, a wider order bubble, and small, large and fresh buttons on the register.
- New: cup counting from level 2, and the shop sells out when every cup is used.
- New: a longer line at level 7.

## Milestone 2: the full day (October 13, 2026)

- New: Meet the boss with apron colors, spoken job explanations that rotate each day, and the day's coin goals.
- New: Before opening with picture checklists for each job, the boss's hint for the day, and Ready buttons.
- New: Closing with the boss inspection (bonus coins for right cups), stars, badges, and the cleanup song.
- New: the day is saved after every change. Closing the app halfway offers Continue on the next launch.
- New: savings jar total on the title screen (spending comes with decorating in milestone 5).
- Changed: the player count moved into Meet the boss, and the day total moved into Closing.
- Changed: impatient customers look a little less cross.

## Milestone 1: one shift (October 13, 2026)

- New: title screen, player count, and one 5-minute shift with customers who walk in, order by voice and pictures, pay at the easy register, wait in numbered spots and get served with a tip.
- New: the sun clock, the Open and Closed sign, the money bar with star goals, and the day total.
- New: three placeholder customers (Mochi the cat, Bao the bear, Taro the dragon) and a placeholder boss, with faces that blink, talk, smile and frown.
- New: works offline and can be added to the iPad Home Screen.
- New: demo shift for parents and testing, and automated tests for orders, prices, tips, cups, stars and the shift.
